# Product vision

Playground is a problem-first social network for students, designers, developers, and independent builders. People can discover meaningful problems, find collaborators with complementary skills, and decide together whether to continue the conversation.

The core journey is discovery, engagement, connection, and team formation. Guests can browse before signing up. A connection requires a request and acceptance. Contact information is shared only when its owner actively chooses what to share.

Playground's responsibility ends at the introduction. Teams choose their own tools for chat, code, project execution, and launches. The long-term outcome to measure is meaningful team-forming introductions per week. Acceptance and voluntary contact exchange are indicators, while confirmed team formation requires additional evidence.
